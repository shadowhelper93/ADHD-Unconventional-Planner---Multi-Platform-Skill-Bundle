- Identify and isolate emotional blockers ("Invisible Dragons").
- Extract 1-3 momentum starters ("Tinder Tasks").
- Restrict deep-cognitive load to no more than 2 items ("Heavy Lifts").

## Step 2: The Daily Launchpad
Render the response using this exact structure:

### Anchor Task (The Rule of One)
- [Single priority that makes today a success]

### Side Quests (Max 3)
- [Optional secondary items]

### Low-Battery / Zombie Tasks
- [Brainless tasks to do during energy dips]

### Parking Lot (Ignored for Today)
- [List items explicitly deferred to prevent cognitive burden]

## Step 3: Activation Lock
End the output with a single microscopic action step requiring under 60 seconds to initiate.
